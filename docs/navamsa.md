# Navamsa (D9) Chart Analysis & Methodology

## 1. Overview of D9 Navamsa in Vedic Astrology

In the classical Vedic astrology system (Parashari framework), the **Navamsa (D9)** chart is the most fundamental divisional chart (Varga). While the D1 Rashi chart shows the physical macrocosm and general birth positions, the D9 Navamsa chart divides each sign into nine micro-segments to reveal inner strength, potential, and subtle planetary dignity.

---

## 2. Calculation Methodology

1. **Input Data**: Sidereal Lahiri longitudes for the Ascendant and planets derived from Swiss Ephemeris.
2. **Division**: 30° / 9 = 3° 20' per division.
3. **Sign Alignment**:
   - **Chara (Movable)** signs align starting from their own sign.
   - **Sthira (Fixed)** signs align starting from the 9th sign.
   - **Dwisvabhava (Dual)** signs align starting from the 5th sign.
4. **House Assignment**: Whole Sign Houses with D9 Lagna as House 1.

---

## 3. Difference Between D1 Rashi and D9 Navamsa

| Feature | D1 Rashi Chart | D9 Navamsa Chart |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Division** | 30° per sign | 3° 20' per division (1/9th of a sign) |
| **Focus** | Physical existence, outer reality, initial placements | Subtlety, micro-dignity, inherent strength |
| **House System** | Equal / Whole Sign / Sripati | Whole Sign based on D9 Lagna |
| **Vargottama** | Reference sign | Sign match check against D1 |

---

## 4. Vargottama Concept

When a planet occupies the exact same sign in both the D1 Rashi chart and the D9 Navamsa chart, it is termed **Vargottama** ("the best division").

In this application:
- **Ascendant Vargottama**: Occurs when D1 Lagna sign = D9 Lagna sign.
- **Planetary Vargottama**: Occurs when D1 planet sign = D9 planet sign.
- All detections are fully factual, deterministic, and verifiable.
