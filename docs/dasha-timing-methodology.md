# Dasha Timeline & Evidence Mapping Methodology

## Vimshottari Hierarchy Integration

The engine leverages `@vedica/dasha-engine` to build nested periods:
- **Mahadasha**: Major 6-to-20 year periods based on Moon's birth nakshatra.
- **Antardasha**: Sub-periods dividing Mahadasha by planetary proportional weights.
- **Pratyantardasha**: Sub-sub-periods providing micro-level timing windows.

## Life Domain Mapping

Each Dasha lord is evaluated against 7 core life domains:
- **CAREER**: 10th house/lord, Saturn, Sun, D10 Dashamsha.
- **WEALTH**: 2nd/11th/5th/9th houses, Jupiter.
- **RELATIONSHIPS**: 7th house/lord, Venus.
- **HEALTH**: 1st/6th/8th/12th houses, Sun, Moon.
- **EDUCATION**: 4th/5th houses, Mercury, Jupiter.
- **PROPERTY**: 4th house, Mars.
- **SPIRITUALITY**: 9th/12th houses, Ketu, Jupiter.
