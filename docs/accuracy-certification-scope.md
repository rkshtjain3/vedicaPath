# Accuracy Certification Scope & Disclaimers

## Certification Wording Standard
Formal accuracy certification reports generated by `@vedica/validation` MUST use the standardized scope statement:

> *"Vedica calculations demonstrated agreement with the independently verified benchmark references under the specified calculation configuration."*

The report MUST NEVER make broad or unqualified claims such as *"Vedica astrology calculations are 100% accurate."*

---

## Required Report Elements
1. **Certification Scope & Wording**
2. **Calculation Configuration** (Ayanamsha, Zodiac, Ephemeris Version, House System, Node Type)
3. **Number of Registered Cases**
4. **Number of Cases With References**
5. **Number of Verified Cases**
6. **Number of Executed Cases**
7. **Pass Count, Fail Count, Incomparable Count**
8. **Maximum, Mean, Median, and RMS Angular Differences**
9. **Component Coverage Breakdown**
10. **Reference Dataset Version** (`chart-reference-dataset-v2`)
11. **Generation Timestamp**

---

## Explicit Exclusions & Disclaimers
1. **Unvalidated Exclusions**: Cases with `NOT_AVAILABLE` status are explicitly excluded from accuracy statistics and pass rates.
2. **Configuration Limits**: Accuracy agreement applies strictly to the tested calculation configuration.
3. **Non-Predictive Disclaimer**: Astronomical calculation agreement verifies mathematical fidelity ONLY. It does NOT validate astrological interpretation or predictive claims.
