# Beginner's Guide: Jagannatha Hora (JHora) Shadbala Benchmarking

This step-by-step guide is written for anyone (no astrology or coding knowledge required) to validate the precision of the `@vedica/shadbala-engine` against **Jagannatha Hora 8.0** (or higher).

---

## What is Shadbala Benchmarking?

Shadbala is a classical Vedic system that calculates planetary strength using 6 main components (Sthana, Dig, Kaala, Cheshta, Naisargika, and Drik Bala). To ensure our software is 100% mathematically accurate, we copy the exact numbers generated by JHora and paste them into our developer benchmark dashboard at `/benchmark/shadbala`.

---

## Step 1: Open Jagannatha Hora (JHora)

1. Launch **Jagannatha Hora** on your Windows desktop (or via WINE/VM on Mac/Linux).
2. Ensure you are using **Lahiri Ayanamsha** and **Sidereal Zodiac** (default JHora settings).

---

## Step 2: Open the Developer Benchmark Dashboard

1. Open your web browser and navigate to:
   ```text
   http://localhost:3000/benchmark/shadbala
   ```
2. Select a benchmark test case from the left sidebar (e.g., **CASE-001**).

---

## Step 3: Enter Birth Details into JHora

1. On the benchmark page, find the **Birth Details** card for the selected case.
2. Click the **COPY BIRTH DETAILS** button. This copies the exact birth parameters:
   - Date of Birth (YYYY-MM-DD)
   - Time of Birth (HH:MM:SS)
   - City / Country
   - Latitude & Longitude
   - Time Zone
3. In JHora, press `Ctrl + B` (or go to `Edit` -> `Birth Data`) and enter the copied values exactly into JHora's fields.
4. Click **OK** in JHora to compute the chart.

---

## Step 4: Confirm JHora Settings Checklist

On our benchmark page, check all 6 items in the **JHora Settings Checklist**:
- [x] Same birth date
- [x] Same birth time
- [x] Same location
- [x] Same timezone
- [x] Sidereal Zodiac
- [x] Lahiri Ayanamsha

> [!IMPORTANT]
> benchmarking comparisons are disabled until all 6 checklist boxes are confirmed.

---

## Step 5: Locate Shadbala Values in JHora

1. In JHora, click on the **Strengths** tab at the top.
2. Click on the **Shadbala** sub-tab.
3. You will see a table displaying planets (`Sun`, `Moon`, `Mars`, `Mercury`, `Jupiter`, `Venus`, `Saturn`) and strength values in **Virupas** or **Rupas**.

---

## Step 6: Enter Numbers into Our Dashboard

1. In our dashboard under **Planet Reference Data Entry**, locate the planet cards (`Sun`, `Moon`, `Mars`, etc.).
2. Type the numbers displayed in JHora into the corresponding component input fields:
   - **Uchcha Bala**
   - **Ojayugma Bala**
   - **Kendradi Bala**
   - **Drekkana Bala**
   - **Dig Bala**
   - **Naisargika Bala**
3. **Leave any missing or uncalculated component fields completely empty.**
   - *Note: An empty field will NOT count as zero. It strictly remains `NOT VALIDATED`.*

---

## Step 7: Read the Live Comparison Results

As soon as you enter a number, the system automatically compares JHora's value with our calculated actual value:

- **Expected**: The JHora number you entered.
- **Actual**: The number computed by our TS engine.
- **Difference**: Mathematical distance $| \text{Actual} - \text{Expected} |$.
- **Tolerance**: Maximum allowable floating point discrepancy (default = `0.05 Virupas`).
- **Status Outcome**:
  - `PASS`: Difference is within allowable tolerance.
  - `FAIL`: Difference exceeds tolerance limit.
  - `NOT VALIDATED`: Field is left empty.

---

## Step 8: Understanding the Benchmark Summary Status

At the top of the page, the **Overall Benchmark Status** is calculated:

- **`NOT VALIDATED`**: No reference values entered yet.
- **`PARTIALLY VALIDATED`**: Some values entered, and all entered values match (`PASS`).
- **`VALIDATED`**: All 42 required component fields entered and all match (`PASS`).
- **`MISMATCH FOUND`**: At least one entered value failed the tolerance comparison (`FAIL`).

---

## Step 9: Investigating a Mismatch (`FAIL`)

If a `FAIL` occurs:
1. Scroll down to the **Failure Investigation** section.
2. Select a **Possible Mismatch Cause** from the dropdown (e.g., `LONGITUDE_BOUNDARY`, `AYANAMSHA`, `FORMULA_VARIANT`).
3. Add optional investigation notes.
4. Click **Save Benchmark Data**.

---

## Step 10: Exporting & Importing Benchmark Data

- Click **EXPORT BENCHMARK JSON** to download a backup file of all entered reference data.
- Click **IMPORT BENCHMARK JSON** to restore or load benchmark data on another machine.
